My approach

Using AI is easy. Mastering it is a craft.

Anyone can open an AI tool. The hard part lies elsewhere: knowing what it is for in your company, what data it is given, who checks its answers and who remains accountable for the decision. That is where I work, between your teams, your business and the technology.

My trade is still management and IT, and AI is now part of it. But a tool is only worth what people make of it: they are the ones who create your company’s added value. That is why I also use coaching, hypnosis and improv to support change.

  • BusinessManagement and IT, the core of what Wize does
  • AIUses that are chosen, framed and checked
  • PeopleTeams that understand, buy in and decide

Management and IT

I work where management and technology meet: projects, organisation, information systems. AI comes in when it brings a real gain, never on principle.

  • Project delivery and change management
  • Organisation, processes and information systems
  • AI built into your processes and tools

Framing AI

Before adopting a tool, you need to know why, with what data and under whose responsibility. I help you choose the right uses and set rules your teams understand.

  • Choosing use cases and assessing risks
  • Usage rules and human oversight of the decisions that matter
  • GDPR and the EU AI Act taken into account from the design stage

Supporting people

Change only works if people carry it. Coaching helps you step back; improv trains a team to listen and adapt.

  • Coaching for leaders and managers
  • Improv workshops and corporate shows
  • Hypnosis, on a voluntary basis and outside any medical setting
