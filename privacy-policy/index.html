Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Data controller

WIZE SRL, Allée des Chanterelles 14, 5101 Erpent, Belgium. VAT BE0691653847. Email: info@wize.be.

2. Scope

This policy covers the websites wize.be and wize.cloud, the client area of the Wize cloud platform (my.wize.cloud) and the services we provide to our clients (consulting, coaching, workshops, website creation, hosting and maintenance).

3. What data we collect

When you contact us by email or phone: your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message.

When you are a client of the Wize cloud platform:

  • your organisation: name, company or self-employed status, company number, VAT number, address and chosen plan
  • the people with access to the client area: name, email address and preferred language
  • your websites: name and address
  • your requests: title, text, page concerned, urgency, and the photos and files you send with them (images or PDF, up to 5 per request)
  • our exchanges: request history, messages, emails sent to you, quotes and items to invoice
  • when you accept a quote: the name you type, the date and time, your IP address, your browser type and a fingerprint of the accepted quote, kept as proof of the agreement
  • to sign in: a single-use link sent by email, valid for 15 minutes, then a session that expires after 90 days without a visit

On wize.be and wize.cloud: these websites have no forms and collect no personal data through automated means beyond what is technically necessary for them to work.

4. Purpose

We process your personal data to answer your questions, to provide our services, to handle your requests and quotes, to invoice, to keep your client area secure and to maintain our business relationship with you.

5. Legal basis

We process your data based on: (a) the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures (client area, requests, quotes, hosting), (b) our legal obligations, in particular accounting, (c) our legitimate interest in answering your questions, keeping proof of agreements and securing our services, and (d) your consent when you contact us on your own initiative.

6. Artificial intelligence

To help us understand and sort the requests sent through the client area, and to draft the explanations we send back, we use the AI service of Anthropic (Claude), based in the United States. Only the text of the request and its context (title, website, page, language) are sent to it: not your name, your email address, nor your photos or files. Every result is checked by a person at Wize before being used, and no decision is made automatically. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

7. Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law:

  • messages and business correspondence: for the duration of the business relationship, plus any legal retention period
  • client area data (requests, photos, exchanges, quotes and their acceptance): for the duration of the contract, then deleted within a reasonable time, except what we must keep as proof or by law
  • accounting documents: for the legal period (currently ten years in Belgium)
  • sign-in links: 15 minutes; sessions: 90 days after the last visit

8. Sharing and processors

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal data. We share it only with the providers who help us run our services, bound by a data processing agreement:

  • Microsoft Azure (Western Europe region): hosting of our websites, of the client area and of its database, and sending of emails
  • Anthropic (United States): analysis of the text of requests, as described in section 6
  • GitHub (Microsoft): storage of the source code of the websites we build and maintain

When data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, it is protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

9. Your website visitors

For the websites we host for our clients, the client remains the controller of the personal data of its visitors and Wize acts as its processor. A data processing agreement is available on request.

10. Your rights under the GDPR

As a data subject in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict the processing of your data
  • receive your data in a structured format (portability)
  • object to certain types of processing
  • withdraw your consent at any time, when processing is based on it
  • lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (www.dataprotectionauthority.be)

To exercise these rights, write to info@wize.be.

11. Cookies

wize.be and wize.cloud use no cookies and no trackers. See our cookie policy. The client area my.wize.cloud only uses two cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work: one keeps you signed in (90 days), the other remembers your language (one year). It contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

12. Hosting and security

Our websites and the client area are hosted by Microsoft Azure in the European Union. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS. The hosting provider may temporarily record technical logs (IP address, date, page requested, browser type) for security purposes. Each client only sees the data of its own organisation, and only Wize staff have access to the administration.

13. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are published on this page with a new revision date.

14. Contact

For any question about this policy or your personal data: WIZE SRL, info@wize.be, +32 475 60 50 35.
