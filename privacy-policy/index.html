2. What data we collect

When you contact us by email or phone, we may collect your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. This website has no forms and collects no personal data through automated means beyond what is technically necessary for it to work.

3. Purpose

We process your personal data only to answer your questions, to provide our services (management consulting, coaching and team workshops) and to maintain our business relationship with you.

We process your data based on: (a) your consent when you contact us, (b) the performance of a contract or pre-contractual measures, and (c) our legitimate interest in answering your questions.

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. Business correspondence is usually kept for the duration of the business relationship plus any legal retention period.

We do not sell, trade or otherwise transfer your personal data to third parties. We may share data with service providers who help us run our business, provided they keep it confidential.

  • lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (www.dataprotectionauthority.be)

To exercise these rights, write to info@wize.be.
