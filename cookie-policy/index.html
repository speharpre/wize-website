1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work and can give information to the site owner.

2. Cookies on this website

This website uses no cookies at all. It does not store anything on your device and contains no analytics, advertising, social media or other third-party trackers.

3. Consent

Because no cookies are used, no consent banner is needed. We publish this policy for transparency.

4. Changes
